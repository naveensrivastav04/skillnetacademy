About SkillNet Academy

A foundation built to close one specific gap

Four things worth knowing before you partner with us, volunteer, or send us your students.

OUR STORY

Where the network started

SkillNet Academy began as a handful of weekend networking labs run out of borrowed classrooms, after one too many hiring partners asked why graduates couldn't do what their own coursework claimed. The network grew college by college, state by state, from that same premise: teach what the next job actually needs.

MISSION & VISION

What we're building toward

Mission: equip every learner we reach with ICT skills that convert directly into work or further study. Vision: an India where a student's postal code no longer predicts their access to a modern, industry-relevant education.

LEADERSHIP

Who runs the network

A small team spanning CSR program delivery, industry-academia partnerships, and government skilling missions leads day-to-day operations, working directly with each partner institution's own faculty rather than around them.

GOVERNANCE

How we stay accountable

An independent board of trustees oversees the foundation's direction and finances, with regular third-party review of program outcomes and fund utilisation shared with every partner and donor.

The linkage model

One network, four pillars, no dead ends

SkillNet Academy doesn't sit beside the ecosystem — it's the connective layer inside it, routing demand signals from industry back into curriculum, and routing learners forward into placement.

Government

Policy alignment with national skilling missions and state skill development corporations.

Academia

Colleges and universities that host labs, faculty, and credit-linked coursework.

Industry

Employers that shape curricula, run live projects, and hire directly from the network.

Learners & Educators

Students and faculty whose progress data keeps every track current and job-relevant.

How the matching works — learner progress, faculty feedback and employer skill demand all feed the same dashboard, so a curriculum gap surfaces in weeks, not admission cycles.
